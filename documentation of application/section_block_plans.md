# Block Plans Register

## 1. Executive Summary
The Block Plans section acts as the official ledger and master registry for all generated maintenance schedules. It provides complete transparency and auditability for every operational disruption planned on the network, transitioning AI-generated concepts into legally approved operational directives.

## 2. Core Functionality & Features
This module is a highly detailed, tabular interface that manages the lifecycle of a scheduled plan:
- **Status Lifecycle Management**: Plans exist in three primary states: `DRAFT` (generated by AI, pending review), `APPROVED` (locked in for execution), or `REJECTED` (sent back for re-planning).
- **Deep Drill-Down**: Clicking on a plan reveals the exact anatomy of the schedule. It breaks down the overall plan into specific "Block Windows" (e.g., Block 1: 02:00-04:00 at KM 15).
- **Task Traceability**: Underneath each block window, it displays every individual maintenance task assigned to that specific time slot, showing the task ID, department, exact location, and timeline sequence.
- **Approval Workflow**: Allows authorized users (Controllers) to formally sign off on a plan, which triggers downstream notifications to field engineers that their track time is approved.

## 3. User Flow & Practical Use Case
**Who uses it:** Chief Controllers, Station Masters, and Audit Officials.
**How it is used:**
1. After the AI Planner generates a schedule, the Controller navigates to the Block Plans register.
2. They open the newly generated `DRAFT` plan.
3. They drill down into the specific blocks to manually verify that the AI's logic is sound. They check the traceability to ensure high-priority tasks are included.
4. If everything looks correct, they click "Approve Plan". The system locks the plan, and it becomes the official operational directive for the week.

## 4. In-Depth AI Integration
While this section is primarily for human review, it utilizes **Explainable AI (XAI)** to build trust between the human operator and the machine:
- **Natural Language Justification**: Because the CP-SAT solver uses complex mathematics to group tasks, a human might not immediately understand *why* two tasks were scheduled at 2 AM instead of 4 AM. The AI backend automatically generates a natural-language "Explanation string" for every single block it creates.
- **Example XAI Output**: "Block BW-001: INTEGRATED block for ENGG and SNT departments. Tasks TMS-001 and SMS-002 merged because they overlap at KM 15.5-18.0 and fit within the 02:00-04:00 shadow window, saving 2.5 hours of block time." This transparency ensures the human controller is never blindly trusting a "black box" algorithm.
